# Project Toolpath pivot — 2026-08-03

## Intent
Pivot the existing Advanced precision-manufacturing marketing site into a browser-based precision-manufacturing game with a credible path toward Steam and Meta Quest VR.

## Outcome
- Replaced the marketing site with a playable, owner-only browser prototype under the temporary working title **Project Toolpath**.
- Shipped one contract, **Emergency Drive Plate**, with stock removal, target geometry, spindle and feed controls, heat/load/tool-wear simulation, chatter/tool-break failure, inspection, grading, payout, retry, local progression, synthesized machine audio, keyboard/pointer/touch controls, reduced-motion support, and a simulation disclaimer.
- Added a cinematic generated social card and kept gameplay assets code-rendered.
- Published Sites version 4 to `https://advanced-precision.advanced-pmd-7340.chatgpt.site`.

## Decisions
- Keep Project Toolpath as a working title pending a future creative approval gate.
- Validate the economic and machining loop in 2D browser form before investing in 3D/VR production.
- Preserve platform portability by separating deterministic simulation rules from the React presentation layer.
- Keep this prototype owner-only; no public release or telemetry.
