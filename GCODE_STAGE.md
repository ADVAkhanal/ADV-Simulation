# G//CODE Stage module

## Product promise

Turn simplified G-code into kinetic ASCII art in seconds. The module teaches the causal idea behind coordinate motion—commands become paths, paths become form—before introducing industrial terminology.

## Current playable slice

- Three authored programs: Neon Bolt, Signal Heart, and Crown Loop.
- Editable simplified commands: G00, G01, G90, G91, X, Y, F, M03, M05, and M30.
- Beat-stepped execution with active-line tracking.
- Realtime ASCII rasterization, motion energy, errors, scoring, reset, pause, and single-step.
- Copyable text performance designed to survive social platforms without an image format.
- Desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard text editing, accessible labels, and reduced-motion handling.
- Renderer-independent deterministic engine emitting canonical motions with source-line provenance and categorized diagnostics.

## Learning ladder

1. Remix one coordinate and observe the changed shape.
2. Finish a partially authored icon.
3. Recreate a target silhouette under a block budget.
4. Use incremental positioning to create repeated motifs.
5. Debug a broken program through single-block mode.
6. Compose an original toolpath performance and share its text artifact.

## Safety boundary

This is a fictional creative sandbox. Its simplified lesson envelope, Z motion, tools, stock removal, alarms, and cycle estimates are game systems rather than validated machine behavior. It does not model workholding, full collision, compensation, real tooling physics, safe feeds/speeds, controller fidelity, or production-ready output. No export to machine-control formats is provided.

The parser and canonical-motion layer are isolated from React so future presentations can reuse deterministic results. This boundary is an architectural proof, not a claim that the TypeScript lesson engine should become an industrial verifier.

## Mainstream strategy

- The hook is visual transformation, not vocational obligation.
- Lessons produce recognizable icons in under one minute.
- Every concept has a shareable artifact and remix prompt.
- Creator challenges use deterministic source text, making remixes attributable and comparable.
- Short-form capture should show code, active line, and resulting ASCII together in a portrait-safe composition.

## Campaign upgrade

- Three client contracts grade geometry coverage, precision, waste, and cycle time.
- The creative controller supports G00/G01 lines, G02/G03 I/J arcs, X/Y/Z positioning, G20/G21 units, G90/G91 modes, spindle and coolant state, and tool selection.
- An ASCII stock-removal map distinguishes rapid motion from material removed below Z0.
- Controller alarms block unsafe below-stock feed motion with the spindle stopped, invalid arcs, and travel-envelope violations.
- Passing inspection awards device-local XP and contract completion. No account, telemetry, or industrial accuracy claim is involved.
