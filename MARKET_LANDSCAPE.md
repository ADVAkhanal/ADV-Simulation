Project Toolpath must not present fictional game values as real feeds, speeds, tolerances, or operating guidance. An eventual educational mode would need validated sources, units, assumptions, provenance, and professional review.

## White-space positioning

**Project Toolpath sits between a tactile machining game and a serious shop drama.**

It combines four layers that competitors usually separate:

1. **Hands:** satisfying, readable material removal.
2. **Head:** tool, route, load, finish, and tolerance decisions.
3. **Shop:** job queue, machine capacity, tooling cost, deadlines, and reputation.
4. **Quality:** inspection evidence, nonconformance decisions, and first-article release.

The differentiation is not maximum realism. It is **consequential manufacturing**: every cut changes schedule, cost, quality, and customer trust.

## Features worth adapting—not copying

- Target-shape clarity and tactile finishing from casual carving games.
- Step-through cause/effect visualization from CNC simulators.
- Transparent recommendations and offline reliability from machinist calculators.
- Material × tool × operation combinations from feeds-and-speeds apps.
- Structured inspection, findings, and open-item closure from aerospace QA apps.
- Machine states, capacity, and deadline pressure from shop-floor systems.

Do not copy proprietary UI, databases, tool recommendations, forms, branded machine controls, screenshots, store copy, or certification content.

## Recommended product sequence

1. Three-contract machining slice with three tool identities.
2. Active inspection: choose characteristics, probe/measure, understand rejection.
3. Small job board: choose between safe, rush, and high-margin contracts.
4. Shop consequences: tooling expense, machine downtime, late delivery, reputation.
5. Data-driven contract generation and daily seeded challenge.
6. Optional code/process view for advanced players using fictionalized, clearly labeled values.
7. Desktop/Steam demo, followed by an inspection-first OpenXR experiment.

## Commercial hypothesis

- Free browser demo: one machine and the Emergency Drive Plate.
- Premium desktop game: complete campaign, workshop progression, and challenge mode.
- Mobile companion or condensed edition only if controls and session design validate; avoid forced ads and pay-to-remove-failure mechanics.
- Training/enterprise licensing is a separate future product requiring validated content and should not distort the entertainment game.
