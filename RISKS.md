# Risks

- Cutting may feel like painting cells rather than machining. Mitigation: validate input, audio, load feedback, and workpiece deformation before higher-fidelity geometry.
- Authenticity can overwhelm onboarding. Mitigation: progressive realism and plain-language assisted mode first.
- Browser prototype architecture could be overextended. Mitigation: keep it a validation client and isolate deterministic rules/content.
- Brand/title may prematurely constrain the product. Mitigation: working title only until founder approval.
- Production machine assets and audio are expensive. Mitigation: code-rendered prototype, provenance ledger, and exact recording plans when physical capture becomes necessary.
- Steam/Quest requirements may change. Mitigation: verify official requirements at each platform milestone and do not claim approval.
