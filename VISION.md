# Vision

Working title: **Project Toolpath**. This is not a final public brand.

Create the definitive precision-manufacturing game for a general gaming audience. The player turns raw material into valuable objects, masters increasingly powerful machines, and grows a workshop into an industrial legacy.

The first proof is a browser vertical slice: accept a contract, cut a recognizable part, experience useful failure, inspect the result, receive a score, and want to try again.

Principles: entertainment first; satisfying within one minute; progressive realism; authentic outcomes; products instead of worksheets; memorable, recoverable failure; reusable simulation rules for web, desktop, and later VR.
